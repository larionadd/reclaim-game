# RECLAIM — Ukrainian reconstruction sandbox prototype

Playable public build: https://larionadd.github.io/reclaim-game/

## Current prototype (November 2026)

The player begins at the manor with one animal farm and one cage. The office PC contains monthly state applications (authored batches on days 1, 31, and 61), the ATBЇB shop with a cart and animal feed, and the Promin marketplace. Workers assigned to the farm produce meat, milk, cheese, and eggs when feed is available. The player chooses each worker's daily food from owned stocks. Listed farm products sell at the end of the day; their proceeds are attributed to the workers who produced them. The day phase indicator advances through the four phases, and bedroom sleep completes the day. The sandbox has no final day.

All 15 base traits affect production, injury risk, health, trust, or wellbeing. The vet post is visible in navigation before construction and needs a hired medic to operate. A hired medic is assigned automatically when the post is built; staff can also be selected on the clinic screen. There is one farm; other enterprises receive player-chosen names. Their production loops, unlimited monthly candidate generation, full enterprise production loops and story arcs are planned but not playable yet. Basic medical modification proposals and operations are playable in the worker file.

The interface supports Ukrainian, Russian, and English; state is saved in local storage with migration from older versions. Run `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, `node scripts/smoke.mjs`, `node scripts/farm-market-smoke.mjs`, `node scripts/vet-traits-smoke.mjs`, `node scripts/manor-smoke.mjs`, `node scripts/worker-profile-smoke.mjs`, and `node scripts/risk-smoke.mjs` to verify the current build.

Workers now receive either wages or food from the player's stores for each shift. The provision type can be changed in the personnel file. The morning report records each worker's health change, provisions received, and reasons such as housing, work, hunger, low pay, or injury. The office lets the player call a worker for rewards, disciplinary actions, or conversation; respect, fear, friendship, and love gate social choices. Intimate scenes remain unavailable while the player directly employs the worker. Housing cards show their full images on phone and desktop. Verify these flows with `node scripts/office-report-smoke.mjs`.


The manor's housing tab is named Settlement. Cage and hut art have new landscape versions with intact bed geometry. Moving a worker into a hut immediately raises trust and respect by 5, friendship by 3, and lowers fear by 2; the daily stay also raises trust and respect. The opposite move harms those relationships. The worker file and office show a new debauchery/openness stat. Adult joke, flirt, and romance talk have visible relationship thresholds. Office interactions and housing moves show a narrative outcome with exact stat changes and are saved to the manor Journal. Verify with `node scripts/journal-interaction-smoke.mjs`.

Workers also have a submission stat, migrated into existing saves. Fear unlocks the order to stand at attention at 20; forced dancing needs fear 35 and submission 15; a demanded apology needs fear 50 and submission 30. These coercive actions increase fear and submission but reduce respect, friendship, or reputation. Office choices and journal entries carry 😇 or 😈 labels; mixed effects show both. Verify with `node scripts/submission-smoke.mjs`.

The office now has a separate Call an NPC tab: choose a worker portrait card before their interaction actions appear. The bedroom has its own call button and portrait cards for NPCs with fear or love above 70. Fear can open nonsexual disciplinary actions for active workers. Intimacy choices are fade-to-black scenes for adult former workers only, after a separate consent request accepted for the current day; high fear never grants consent. Verify with `node scripts/bedroom-flow-smoke.mjs`.

Each personnel file now includes age, orientation, marital status, and mother, father, and children records. A known relative has a name; an existing but unidentified relative is marked as unknown. Candidate applications state whether modification consent was recorded at signing. The player can propose a procedure, and accepted procedures cost the worker's own savings, add recovery time, and appear in the journal. Medical grants were removed. Older saves transfer previously assigned grants into the worker's savings during migration. Fear alone produces a refused, reputation-damaging proposal rather than medical consent. Verify with `node scripts/npc-personal-modifications-smoke.mjs`.


The veterinary post now hosts the procedure list after construction and appointment of a living medic. New procedures include eye replacement, separate left/right arm and leg replacements, breast replacement/enlargement, a fictional oviposition uterus, and repeatable lactation, hair-growth, hair-loss, and weight-gain hormone courses. Each procedure lists its price, recovery days, combined complication/injury risk, and death risk. Individual body slots cannot be replaced twice, while courses can repeat without a global operation limit. A successful procedure applies body or production effects; complication extends recovery; severe injury permanently reduces capacity; death closes the contract. Every outcome is written to the worker's file and journal. The worker pays from personal savings, and proposals still require consent. Verify with `node scripts/medical-outcomes-smoke.mjs`.

Candidate applications now include individual letters and requested wages. The player can make a lower counteroffer; offers below each candidate's minimum are refused. Guilt and defiance influence unauthorized absence and refusal to work. These incidents cancel the shift's output and pay, appear in the daily report and journal, and can be handled through existing disciplinary actions. Successful oviposition or lactation modifications unlock separate assignments after recovery. They produce premium hen-girl eggs (45 ₴ each) and cow-girl milk (36 ₴ each), with separate inventory, images, and Promin listings. Save version 14 migrates older inventories and workers. Verify with `node scripts/letters-disobedience-premium-smoke.mjs`.

The office intimacy proposal button opens when love or fear exceeds 80. A high-fear proposal is refused and harms trust, respect, and reputation; the proposal does not start an intimacy scene. Verify with `node scripts/office-proposal-threshold-smoke.mjs`.

The manor bedroom and office now have distinct generated interior artwork on their room cards and destination screens. Verify both at mobile width with `node scripts/manor-room-art-smoke.mjs`.

All 18 clinic procedures now render as illustrated responsive cards; each image path lives in the procedure JSON. The illustrations are reproducible with `python scripts/generate_procedure_art.py`. Verify mobile image loading with `node scripts/clinic-card-art-smoke.mjs`.

Successful modifications appear as named states in the NPC file. Mechanical prosthetics are sold at ATBЇB, kept in storage, issued to an NPC, and fitted or removed in their inventory. Three additional adult candidates have portraits, application letters, and personal records; Mei Chen is a medic available from day one. The office offers three fade-to-black intimacy choices for adult former workers after separate same-day consent. Numeric relationship thresholds remain in effect; fear does not create consent.

Local setup: Node.js 20.19+ or 22.12+, then `npm install` and `npm run dev`. See [GDD.md](./GDD.md) and [PORTRAIT_STANDARD.md](./PORTRAIT_STANDARD.md).
